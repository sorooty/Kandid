# Offer sheet and fit

## `fiche.md`

Write it in the user's language, plain Markdown, at most one screen:

- Company, role, contract, duration and rhythm (for work-study), place and remote policy, start date, salary if given.
- Missions, in a few lines.
- Requirements, split into "indispensable" and "apprécié", with the offer's own words.
- Keywords an ATS would look for (tools, methods, diplomas, languages, soft skills named in the offer).
- How to apply and deadline, if stated.
- Points to check: anything vague, contradictory or unusual (school or intermediary posting, unclear contract, very
  short duration, unpaid internship beyond the legal limit, a requirement that looks out of reach).

## Fit

Compare the requirements with `profile.yaml` only:

- Met: requirement and the profile item that proves it (experience id, project id or skill).
- Partly met: what is close, and why it is not the same thing.
- Not met: say so.

Give a verdict in one line ("bonne adéquation", "adéquation partielle", "faible adéquation") with the two or three
reasons that decide it, then which experiences and projects best carry this application. The verdict is advice: the
user decides.
