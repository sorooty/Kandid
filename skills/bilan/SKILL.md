# Weekly review

1. `kandid bilan --json` (add `--jours <n>` if "$ARGUMENTS" gives a number) and `kandid suivi --json`.
2. Report in a few lines: offers found, applications sent, answers and answer rate, interviews; which sources led to
   applications and interviews.
3. Read the numbers honestly: what seems to work, what does not, and one or two concrete adjustments (keywords to
   add or drop in `config.yaml`, a source to enable, follow-ups that are due). Keep it encouraging and factual.
